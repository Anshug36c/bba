📊 Exam Pattern — samajh lo, phir padho

Theory papers (70 marks ESE — End Semester Exam)

  • University ke external exam me 70 marks ka paper aata hai (3 hours).
  • Section A: Short/very short answers — definitions aur 2-3 line ke sawal (≈20-25 marks).
  • Section B: Medium answers — "explain", "list", "differentiate" wale (≈30-35 marks).
  • Section C: Long answers / numerical / case (≈20-25 marks).
  • Internal 30 marks: Sessional test (10-15) + assignment/presentation (5-10) + attendance/participation (5).

Viva (100 marks — CMM-106P)

  • Face-to-face external examiner ke saath, sem 1 ke kisi bhi topic par.
  • Domain knowledge 40 + Confidence 30 + Communication 30.
  • Poori taiyari: 07_Viva_Preparation.html (self-intro script + 100+ ready answers).

📊 Exam Pattern — 70 + 30 ko samjho

ESE (End Semester Exam) — 70 marks, 3 hours

SectionTypical structureMarks
Section AShort answer / Very short answer — definition, 2-3 line (5-8 questions)~20-25
Section BMedium answer — explain karo, 8-10 line ya points (4-5 questions)~30-35
Section CLong answer / Numerical / Case — detail me (2-3 questions)~20-25

Exact pattern university ke hisaab se thoda badal sakta hai — par strategy same rehti hai: Section A full, Section B 80%, Section C me jo pakka aata hai wo pehle.

Internal (30 marks) kaise bante hain

  • Sessional/Class test: 10-15 marks
  • Assignments / Presentations: 5-10 marks — yahan notes se hi copy karo (syllabus-matching material ready hai)
  • Attendance + Class participation: 5 marks (75% attendance mostly compulsory)
Marks bachane ki 5 golden baatein: (1) Har long answer me headings + points likho, paragraph nahi. (2) Jahan diagram bana sako — banao (Business cycle, CPU, topologies, P&L format, graph) — wo 2 marks pakka deta hai. (3) Example likho (Amul, Tata, Jio, Maruti, UPI) — examiner turant impressed. (4) Numerical me working notes likho — half marks bhi nahi katta. (5) Answer ke end me "conclusion/importance" ki 1-2 line zaroor.

✅ Notes kaise use karein (4 step method)

  1. First read: Unit padho — koi line skip na karo. Boxes ko dhyan se dekho: 📘 definition, 🗣️ Hinglish explanation, 🌍 real example, 🧠 trick, 🧮 formula, 🎯 tip, ⚠️ warning, 📝 exam questions, 🔑 key point.
  2. Written practice: Har unit ke baad 2 long answers likh kar dekho (bina notes dekhe). Jo atke wahi weak point hai.
  3. Rapid revision: Har unit ka dark ⚡ box raat ko padho. Poora semester 30 min me revise ho jaayega.
  4. Exam se pehle: Sirf rapid revision + probable questions + formats. Naya kuch nahi padho (last-minute naye topic se confusion badhta hai).
Real life example — is method ka faayda: Jo student pura semester "notes padhta" hai par likhta nahi — exam me likhne me atakta hai. Jo student har unit ke baad 2 answer likh kar aage badhta hai — uska haath + dimaag dono train ho jaate hain. Accounting me ye difference sabse zyada dikhta hai: journal entry padhne se yaad nahi hoti, khud likhne se hoti hai. Isliye is kit me har unit ke saath "probable questions" diye hain — unko likho! ✍️

UNIT 1 — Introduction to Accounting

1.1 Accounting kya hai? (Meaning & Definition)

Socho tumhare papa ki ek chhoti dukaan hai. Roz subah counter kholte hain, shaam ko band karte hain. Din bhar me kitna maal aaya, kitna bikri hui, kitna udhaar gaya, kitna kharcha hua — agar ye sab kuch likhi hui jagah na ho, to agle mahine unhe ye bhi pata nahi chalega ki faayda hua ya nuksaan. Wahi likhne-rakhne ka systematic kaam Accounting hai.

Accounting = kis logic ki financial transactions ko systematic tarike se record karna, classify karna, summarise karna, analyse karna aur jinke results (profit/loss, financial position) users ko report karna — taaki wo sahi decision le saken.

American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) ke shabdon me:

"Accounting is the art of recording, classifying and summarising in a significant manner and in terms of money, transactions and events which are, in part at least, of a financial character, and interpreting the results thereof." — Yani accounting = recording + classifying + summarising + interpreting.

Accounting ka process (step by step)

Step 1  IDENTIFY  ->  Financial transaction identify karo (voucher aaya)
Step 2  RECORD    ->  JOURNAL me likho (double entry)
Step 3  CLASSIFY  ->  LEDGER me posting karo (account-wise)
Step 4  SUMMARISE ->  TRIAL BALANCE banao
Step 5  FINALISE  ->  Trading a/c + P&L a/c + Balance Sheet
Step 6  ANALYSE   ->  Ratio / trend analysis
Step 7  COMMUNICATE -> Report / Annual report se users tak

1.2 Importance / Need / Objectives & Scope

(A) Objectives of Accounting

  • Record karne ka objective: Har transaction ka systematic record rakhna.
  • Profit/Loss nikalna: Period ka net result (Profit & Loss account se).
  • Financial position batana: Balance Sheet se assets-liabilities ka picture.
  • Property/assets ki jaankari aur un mein kya-kya badla — record rakhna.
  • Decision support: Management, owners, bank, sarkar — sabko sahi info dena.

(B) Importance — kisko kaam aata hai? (Users of accounting information)

UserKya dekhta hai / kyun
Owner / ShareholdersPaisa kamaya ya nahi? Dividend milega? Share ki value badhegi?
ManagementKaunsa product loss me hai? Cost control kahan karna hai?
Banks / LendersLoan diya to wapas milega? Balance sheet + cash flow se check karte hain
Suppliers / CreditorsIs company ko udhaar par maal de sakte hain ya nahi?
Sarkar / Tax DepartmentIncome tax, GST — kitna tax banta hai?
Employees / Labour UnionBonus, increment, job security ka aadhaar
Investors / AnalystsInvest karna chahiye ya nahi — profit trend dekhte hain
Public / SocietyCompany ka contribution, CSR, employment
Real life example: Jab tum home loan ke liye bank jate ho, bank tumhari salary slip ke saath-saath kitni EMI already chal rahi hai ye dekhta hai — matlab bank individual ki bhi accounting dekh raha hai. Aur jab Amul ya Tata ki annual report aati hai, unki audited accounts hi investors ko batati hain ki company kitni strong hai. Isliye kehte hain: "No accounting = No trust = No business."

(C) Scope / Branches of Accounting

BranchKaam kya karta hai
Financial AccountingOverall profit/loss aur financial position — shareholders, banks, tax ke liye
Cost AccountingProduct/kaam ki cost nikalna, cost control, cost reduction — industry ka favourite (Maruti ke kaar me per-unit cost)
Management AccountingManager ke decision ke liye data — budgeting, variance, ratio analysis
Tax AccountingIncome tax, GST, TDS compliance
Social / Human Resource AccountingSamaj aur employees ko value dena (CSR report, employee value)
Limitations of accounting (yahan marks kat jaate hain agar na likho): (1) Sirf financial cheezein, quality/employee morale nahi likhta. (2) Historical record hai — future kal ka kuch nahi batata. (3) Price level changes (inflation) ko ignore karta hai. (4) Window dressing possible hai — achha dikhane ke liye figures ko sundar kiya ja sakta hai. (5) Personal judgement/bias — depreciation method, provision ka estimate. (6) Sirf money value — goodwill internally build hui nahi dikhati.

1.4 Accounting Principles / Concepts / Conventions (GAAP)

Ye "khel ke rules" hain. Inhi ke aadhar par financial statement samajhne layak rehte hain. Isko GAAP (Generally Accepted Accounting Principles) kehte hain.

(A) Key Concepts (basic assumptions — assume karke chalta hai)

ConceptRuleExample
Business Entity ConceptBusiness aur owner alag hain. Owner ka paisa = capital (liability of business)Papa ne dukaan ko ₹5,00,000 daala — dukaan ke books me ye "Capital" hai, papa ki jaib nahi
Money Measurement ConceptSirf paisa me naapa jaane wali cheezein likhoManager ki achhi leadership nahi likhi, lekin uski salary likhi jayegi
Going Concern ConceptBusiness aage bhi chalega aisa maan kar chalta hai — isliye asset ki cost ko saalon me baant dete hain (depreciation)Zameen ki cost ek saal me nahi likh rahe, isliye building 30 saal me depreciate hoti hai
Cost ConceptAsset ko uske purchase price par likho, market price par nahiVikas ne ₹8,00,000 me flat kharida; aaj market ₹12,00,000 hai — books me 8L hi rahega
Dual Aspect ConceptHar transaction ka do taraf effect — Debit = Credit. Yahi Accounting Equation ka base haiCash laaye ₹1,00,000 — Cash badha (Dr.) aur Capital badha (Cr.)
Realisation ConceptIncome tabhi maano jab bikri ho chuki ho, promise nahiCustomer ne bola "kal kharidunga" — ye sale nahi
Accrual ConceptIncome/expense us period me likho jab wo hua, chahe paisa aaya/gaaya ho ya nahiMarch ki salary May me mili — to March me hi expense.

(B) Conventions (practice-based guidelines)

ConventionRule
ConsistencyEk baar jo method chuna (jaise SLM depreciation) use saal-dar-saal follow karo — warna comparison bekaar

1.5 Accounting Equation (sabse zyada use hoti hai)

Assets = Liabilities + Capital
Ya isko aise likho: Capital = Assets − Liabilities
Dual aspect kehta hai: har transaction ke baad bhi ye equation balance rehti hai. Agar koi entry likhne me doubt ho to isi equation se check karo.

Examples — equation kaise kaam karta hai

TransactionEffectAssets=Liabilities + Capital
1. Owner ne business me ₹5,00,000 daalaCash badha, capital badha5,00,000=0 + 5,00,000
2. Bank se loan liya ₹2,00,000Cash badha, liability badhi7,00,000=2,00,000 + 5,00,000
3. Furniture kharida ₹1,00,000 cashEk asset se dusra asset (cash ghata, furniture badha)6,00,000 + 1,00,000 = 7,00,000=2,00,000 + 5,00,000
4. Maal kharida udhaar (credit) par ₹80,000Stock badha, creditors badhe7,80,000=2,80,000 + 5,00,000
5. Owner ne personal use ke liye ₹20,000 nikaleCash ghata, capital ghata (drawing)7,60,000=2,80,000 + 4,80,000
6. Creditors ko ₹30,000 diyeCash ghata, liability ghati7,30,000=2,50,000 + 4,80,000
Doctor wala formula bhi yahi hai: Assets (jo humare paas hai) = Liabilities (jo doosron ka hai) + Capital (jo owner ka hai). Kabhi bhi ye equation toot jaye — samjho entry galat hai.

1.7 Golden Rules of Debit & Credit (D-C ka baap)

Debit (Dr.) = Left side of account. Credit (Cr.) = Right side. Ye "good" aur "bad" nahi hai — sirf side ke naam hain. Har entry me Debit = Credit hota hai.

Traditional rules (account type ke hisaab se)

Account typeRuleExample
PersonalDebit the receiver, Credit the giver (jo letaa hai debit, jo deta hai credit)Ram ko ₹20,000 diye → Ram Dr., Cash Cr.
RealDebit what comes in, Credit what goes outFurniture kharida → Furniture Dr., Cash Cr.
NominalDebit all expenses & losses, Credit all incomes & gainsSalary di → Salary Dr., Cash Cr.
Trick: Personal = "R-G" (Receiver debit, Giver credit) • Real = "IN-OUT" (jo andar aaye debit, jo bahar jaye credit) • Nominal = "Kharcha Debit, Kamai Credit". Bas teen line me poora double entry system set hai ✅

Modern rules (ALCRE) — jo aaj software/Tally me chalta hai

TypeRule
AssetsIncrease → Debit | Decrease → Credit
LiabilitiesIncrease → Credit | Decrease → Debit
CapitalIncrease → Credit | Decrease → Debit
RevenueIncrease → Credit | Decrease → Debit
ExpensesIncrease → Debit | Decrease → Credit

UNIT 2 — Journal, Ledger & Trial Balance

2.1 Journal — "Roznamcha"

Journal = business ki sabse pehli book jis me transactions date-wise, chronological order me double-entry form me likhi jaati hain. Isliye isko Book of Original Entry / Subsidiary Book ka baap / Roznamcha bhi kehte hain.

Format of Journal

Date  | Particulars (Dr/Cr)              | L.F | Debit(₹) | Credit(₹)
------|----------------------------------|-----|----------|----------
Apr 1 | Cash A/c              Dr.        |     | 5,00,000 |
      |    To Capital A/c                |     |          | 5,00,000
      | (Being cash brought in as capital)|   |          |
  • Date — transaction ki tareekh.
  • Particulars — Debit account pehle likho (prefix "Dr."), neeche "To" lagakar credit account; dono ki narration bracket me.
  • L.F. = Ledger Folio (ledger me kis page par posting ki, uska number).
  • Dr./Cr. amount — barabar hona chahiye.
  • Narration — short explanation "Being ..." — ye examiner ko confident dikhata hai.
Steps of journalising: 1) Kaunse do accounts affect hue, pehchano. 2) Unka type (Asset/Liability/Capital/Revenue/Expense) likho. 3) Rule apply karo (debit kaunsa, credit kaunsa). 4) Entry likho + narration do. 5) Check karo: Debit total = Credit total.

Types of Journal entries (aadhi neend me bhi yaad)

Entry typeEntry kaise banti haiExample
Simple entry1 debit + 1 creditCash sale: Cash Dr. To Sales
Compound entryEk hi date par 2 se zyada accountsSalary ₹20,000 + Rent ₹5,000 cash me: Salary Dr. 20,000, Rent Dr. 5,000, To Cash 25,000
Opening entryNaye saal ka pehla entry — pichhle saal ke assets/liabilities aage laanaCash Dr., Stock Dr., Furniture Dr. To Creditors, To Capital
Closing entryPeriod end par nominal accounts band karna (P&L/Trading me transfer)Sales Dr. To Trading A/c
Adjustment entryYear-end adjustment ke liye (outstanding, prepaid, depreciation…)Depreciation Dr. To Machinery
Rectification entryGalat entry sudharne ke liyeSuspense/related account se correct karna
Transfer entryEk account se dusre me transferProfit & Loss A/c Dr. To Capital A/c (profit transfer)

2.2 Ledger — "Khata" (principal book)

Ledger = journal ki entries ko account-wise classify karke rakhi jaane wali book. Isko Principal Book / Book of Final Entry kehte hain — kyunki yahan se hi trial balance aur final accounts bante hain.

Format of Ledger Account (T-format)

Apr 1| To Capital  |      |5,00,000| Apr 3| By Furniture|     |   60,000
Apr 8| To Sales    |      |  40,000| Apr12| By Sohan    |     |1,00,000
Apr25| To Mohit    |      |  45,000| Apr18| By Rent     |     |   15,000
     |             |      |        | Apr18| By Salary   |     |   25,000
     |             |      |        | Apr30| By Balance c/d|   |3,85,000
     |             |      |5,85,000|      |             |     |5,85,000

Posting ke steps

  1. Journal ka debit account dhoondo, uska ledger me debit side likho; "To (credit account ka naam)" likho.
  2. Journal ka credit account dhoondo, uska ledger me credit side likho; "By (debit account ka naam)" likho.
  3. Har posting me J.F. (Journal Folio) likho.
  4. Mahine/period ke end me dono side jodo → farak = balance. "Balance c/d" likh kar dono side barabar karo.
  5. Naye period me "Balance b/d" likh kar shuru karo.

Journal vs Ledger

NarrationZarooriNahi likhi jaati
BalancingNahi hotaHar account balance hota hai

2.3 Trial Balance

Trial Balance = ek statement jis me ek particular date par sab ledger accounts ke debit aur credit balances ki list bana kar dono totals ko barabar dikhaya jaata hai — taaki ye prove ho sake ki double entry me arithmetical accuracy hai.

Objectives / Uses

  • Arithmetical accuracy check karna (Debit total = Credit total).
  • Final accounts banane ka base dena (Trading, P&L, Balance Sheet).
  • Errors detect karna (partially).
  • Ek jagah sab account balances ka summary dena.

Rule: kaunsa account Dr. side, kaunsa Cr. side

Debit balance wale (Dr. column)Credit balance wale (Cr. column)
Cash, Bank, Furniture, Machinery, Building, Stock, Debtors, Drawings, Purchase, Returns inward (Sales Return), Carriage inward, Wages, Salaries, Rent, Insurance, Bad debts, Discount allowed, Expenses/LossesCapital, Loan, Creditors, Bills payable, Sales, Returns outward (Purchase Return), Commission received, Interest received, Discount received, Provision for doubtful debts, Reserve fund, Outstanding expenses
Shortcut: Jo cheez humare paas hai ya humne kharch kiya → Dr. Jo cheez doosron ki hai ya humne kamaya → Cr. Wahi ALCRE rule hai, bas balance side ki shakal me.

Methods of preparing Trial Balance

  1. Totals method — har account ke total (debit total/Credit total) list karo.
  2. Balances method — sirf net balance likho (sabse common).
  3. Totals-cum-balances method — dono.

Errors — konse pakde jaate hain, konse nahi

Rectification of Errors

  • Before Trial Balance: Entry ko seedha sudhar do (kuch cases me journal se).
  • After Trial Balance, difference hua: Pehle Suspense Account kholo, difference daalo, phir ek-ek galti ka rectification entry karke Suspense ko band karo.
  • After final accounts: Agar profit affected hua ho to Profit & Loss Adjustment A/c (previous year adjustment) use karo.

Real life example: NEFT/UPI me paisa galat number par chala gaya — bank turant "reversal entry" karti hai. Ek entry se dusra account adjust hota hai aur ek temporary suspense me difference rehta hai jab tak matter settle na ho. Bilkul yahi Suspense Account ka concept hai. Aur jab railway ya bank ka daily "tally" hota hai (cash counter ka total milaana) — wahi trial balance ka roop hai 👌

⚡ Rapid Revision — Unit 2

  • Journal = Book of Original Entry, date-wise double entry + narration.
  • Ledger = Book of Final Entry (Principal book), account-wise, "To"/"By" format.
  • Posting = journal se ledger me transfer; balance c/d se account band hota hai.
  • Trial Balance = ledger balances ki list; Dr. total = Cr. total (as on date).
  • Dr. side: assets, expenses, drawings, purchase, debtors. Cr. side: liabilities, capital, income, sales, creditors.
  • 4 errors trial balance me pakde nahi jaate: Omission, Commission, Principle, Compensating.
  • Rectification: Trial balance se pehle seedha; baad me Suspense A/c; final accounts ke baad P&L Adjustment A/c.

UNIT 3 — Final Accounts (Trading A/c, P&L A/c, Balance Sheet & Receipts-Payments)

3.1 Final Accounts kya hote hain?

Final Accounts = saal ke end par banaye jaane wale statements jo batate hain: (1) Trading A/c — gross profit/loss, (2) Profit & Loss A/c — net profit/loss, (3) Balance Sheet — ek date par assets & liabilities ki position. Inhi ko financial statements kehte hain.
Hinglish me: Trading A/c = "bikri ka hisaab" (kitna becha, kitne me bechne ke liye laya). P&L A/c = "ghar ke kharchon ka hisaab". Balance Sheet = "aaj ka status" — kitna maal, kitna karza, aur owner ka kitna. Ek baat yaad rakho: Trading aur P&L account "period" ke hote hain (1 April se 31 March tak), aur Balance Sheet "point" ka hota hai (jaise "and as on 31 March").

3.2 Trading Account

Trading Account = wo account jo direct expenses aur net sales/value of goods ka comparison karke Gross Profit ya Gross Loss nikalta hai. Isme manufacturing/trading se seedha juda kharcha aata hai.

3.3 Profit & Loss Account

Profit & Loss A/c = wo account jo indirect expenses, losses, other incomes jodkar Net Profit / Net Loss nikalta hai. Ye Gross Profit se shuru hota hai aur Net Profit ko Capital me transfer karta hai (Balance Sheet me).

Typical items — Debit side (expenses/losses)

  • Office & administrative: Salary, Rent (office), Printing & Stationery, Postage, Telephone, Insurance, Audit fees, Legal charges.
  • Selling & distribution: Advertisement, Carriage outward, Salesman commission, Bad debts, Provision for doubtful debts, Discount allowed, Packing (for delivery).
  • Financial: Interest on loan, Bank charges, Discount on issue of shares, Interest on capital.
  • Maintenance & others: Repairs, Depreciation, Loss by fire/theft (jo insurance se cover nahi hua), Charity/Donation.

Credit side (incomes/gains)

  • Gross Profit (Trading A/c se)
  • Discount received, Commission received, Interest received on investments/deposits
  • Rent received, Dividend received, Profit on sale of old asset, Excess provision written back, Bad debts recovered

UNIT 4 — Depreciation Accounting & Stock Valuation

4.1 Depreciation kya hai?

Tumhare ghar me fridge 10 saal chalega. Uska purana hona — body rust lagna, compressor ka weak hona, resale value girna — ye sab depreciation hai. Company ke books me bhi har fixed asset ki value saal-dar-saal ghanti hai — kyunki wo "use" hota hai.

Depreciation = fixed asset ki cost (ya book value) me se, uske useful life ke aadhaar par, har saal jo decline hota hai usko systematically expense me likhna. Ye asset ke use, wear and tear, ya obsolescence (purana ho jaana) ki wajah se hota hai. AS-6 (Depreciation) isko govern karta hai.

Causes of Depreciation (5 causes — exam me pakka)

CauseMatlabExample
1. Wear and tearRozana use se ghisnaDelivery van ke tyre
2. Efflux of timeSirf samay guzarne se (chahe use na ho)Leasehold land, licence, patents
3. ObsolescenceNai technology ke aane se purana ho jaana2G phone wale ne 4G aane par machine badli
4. AccidentsDurghatna se kharab honaTruck accident me total loss (partly insurance)
5. Depletion exhaustionNatural resource ka khatam honaCoal mine, oil well (ko depletion kehte hain)

Need / Objectives of charging depreciation

  • True profit nikalna — asset ke use ka cost usi saal ke revenue se match karna (matching concept).
  • True financial position — asset ko grip value par dikhana.
  • Replacement fund — paisa jama hoke nai machine kharidne me kaam aaye.
  • Tax benefit — expense hone se taxable profit kam, tax bachta hai.
  • Cost of production sahi nikalna — cost accounting ke liye zaroori.
  • Dividend decision — capital ko dividend me baant dena galat hoga.

Factors (jo calculation me chahiye)

  1. Cost of asset (kharid ki keemat + installation + freight + erection)
  2. Estimated useful life (saal/ghante/units)
  3. Estimated scrap/salvage/residual value
  4. Method of depreciation chunna
Formula (dono method ka base): Depreciable amount = Cost − Scrap value. Yahi amount saari life me expense me likhi jaati hai (chahe kis method se bhi).

4.2 Methods of Depreciation

MethodKaise kaam karta haiKahan best hai
1. Straight Line Method (SLM / Original cost / Fixed instalment)Har saal barabar amount: (Cost − Scrap) ÷ Life. Depreciation asset ki original cost par lagti haiJahan use har saal barabar ho — building, furniture, patents, lease
2. Written Down Value (WDV / Diminishing balance)Har saal book value par fixed % lagta hai; amount har saal girti haiJahan shuru me repair kam, baad me zyada — machinery, vehicles, computers
3. Annuity methodAsset ka "interest" bhi cost me jodte hain — barabar annual chargeLeasehold property, long-term assets
4. Sinking fund methodDepreciation ke saath ek fund me paisa invest karte hain jo replacement ke time kaam aayeBade plants, jinke replacement me bada paisa lage
5. Insurance policy methodPolicy premium ke roop me jama karna; maturity par replacementChhoti firms jahan discipline nahi hoti 😅
6. Depletion / Units of productionProduction/hours ke hisaab se: (Cost − Scrap) × (Units is saal ÷ Total units)Mines, quarries, oil wells
7. Machine hour ratePer hour charge × actual hoursJahan machine ka use uneven ho

4.4 Stock / Inventory Valuation

Stock (Inventory) = business ke paas bechne ke liye pada hua maal — raw material, work-in-progress (WIP) aur finished goods. Stock valuation = saal ke end par is maal ki value lagana — chahe cost par ya market value par, jo bhi kam ho.
AS-2 ka rule (bilkul pakka yaad karo): Inventory ko "Lower of Cost and Net Realisable Value (NRV)" par value karo. NRV = estimated selling price − selling ke kharch (delivery, commission). Matlab: agar market me daam gir gaya hai to loss aaj hi maan lo (conservatism), par agar market daam badha hai to profit tabhi jab bikri ho jaye.

Methods of Stock Valuation

MethodKaam kaise karta haiEffect jab price badh rahi ho
FIFO (First In First Out)Puraana maal pehle bikta hai — closing stock naye (mehnge) maal parClosing stock value zyada → profit zyada dikhta (inflation me)
LIFO (Last In First Out)Naya maal pehle nikala jaata hai — closing stock purane (saste) maal parClosing stock kam → profit kam, tax benefit
HIFO (Highest In First Out)Sabse mehnga maal pehleUsed for tax planning (limited use)
Weighted Average (AVCO)Average cost = Total cost ÷ Total units (periodic) ya har purchase ke baad naya average (moving average)Extremes nikal jate hain — middle path
Specific IdentificationHar unit ko uske actual cost se identity karoJewellery, cars, machines ke liye
Standard costPre-set standard cost par valueManufacturing me control ke liye
Base stock / Adjusted selling priceEk minimum stock always base value parRarely used, theory question ke liye
Note: Indian companies ke financial statements me LIFO allowed nahi hai (AS-2 ke hisaab se FIFO ya Weighted Average chalti hai). LIFO sirf theory/exam aur US-GAAP me milta hai — ye line likhne se examiner impress hota hai👌

Worked example — FIFO vs Weighted Average

Transactions:
1 Apr  Opening stock   100 units @ ₹10  = 1,000
10 Apr Purchase        200 units @ ₹12  = 2,400
20 Apr Purchase        100 units @ ₹15  = 1,500
Total available        400 units       total cost = 4,900
Sale during month      250 units  (closing stock = 150 units)

FIFO (purana pehle gaya):
Sold = 100 units @10 (1,000) + 150 units @12 (1,800) = 2,800
Closing stock = 50 units @12 (600) + 100 units @15 (1,500) = ₹2,100

Weighted Average:
Average cost = 4,900 / 400 = ₹12.25
Closing stock = 150 x 12.25 = ₹1,837.50
Cost of goods sold = 250 x 12.25 = ₹3,062.50

Compare: FIFO closing stock (2,100) > Weighted average (1,837.50)
=> FIFO me zyada profit, FIFO se tax bhi zyada. Isi logic se exam me
   "price badhne par kaunsa method zyada profit dikhayega?" ka answer = FIFO.
Real life example: Kirana dukaan me 2 bori aata aayi — ek ₹1,200 (purani rate) aur ek ₹1,400 (nayi rate, gehu mehnga ho gaya). Jab grahak aata hai, dukandar kehta hai "naya aata ₹1,400 ka". Wo LIFO jaisa hi behaviour hai. Par agar wo pehle purani bori khatam kare, to FIFO. Aur agar wo dono bori ka average nikaal kar ₹1,300 me beche — to Weighted Average. Simple! 👌

6GST complianceGSTR-1/3B ke liye data auto; e-invoice/e-way bill integration
7Cost savingKam staff, kam stationery, kam errors — long run me sasta
8ScalabilityEk dukaan se 100 branch ka data same software me
9Analysis & MISRatio, trend, cost centre report — management decision easy
10Security & audit trailPassword, user roles, koi entry delete kare to trail rehta hai

Limitations / Challenges

  • Setup cost (software + hardware + training) zyada.
  • Staff ko training chahiye — thoda resistance hota hai.
  • Data security & hacking/virus ka risk; regular backup zaroori.
  • System failure/power cut ka risk — UPS aur cloud backup chahiye.
  • GIGO: Garbage In Garbage Out — galat input se galat report.
  • Sab kuch automate ho gaya to basic accounting samajh kamzor ho sakti hai 😅
Real life example: Tumhare area ke dairy shop wale ke paas pehle 3 registers hoti thi (udhaar, cash, stock). Aaj ek laptop me Tally hai — grahak ka naam par udhaar entry, bill print, mahine ka GST summary. Ek hi bandaa pehle se 5 guna kaam ek baar me kar leta hai. Yahi computerised accounting ka jaadu hai 📈

5.2 Tally — Practical Applications

Tally = India ka sabse popular accounting software (Tally Solutions, Bangalore). Poora naam: TallyPrime (pehle Tally.ERP 9, usse pehle Tally 9/7.2). Isme accounting, inventory, GST, payroll, banking, e-way bill — sab ek hi jagah. Ye "Accounting with Inventory" software hai. "Tally" naam isliye — kyunki tally karna = milaana/check karna.

Accounting ke basic concepts Tally me

  • Company Information: create company, company details (name, financial year, address, currency), alter, delete, security control, backup/restore.
  • Groups: Ledger accounts ko group me baantna (28 pre-defined primary/secondary groups). Jaise Sundry Debtors, Sundry Creditors, Bank Accounts, Cash-in-hand, Sales Accounts, Purchase Accounts, Expenses, Capital, Loans.
  • Ledgers: Actual accounts jinme entry ki jaati hai — Capital, Ram (debtor), SBI bank, Rent, Salary, GST Output.
  • Vouchers: Transaction ke prakaar.
  • Display/Reports: Day Book, Ledger, Trial Balance, Trading A/c, P&L A/c, Balance Sheet, Cash/Fund Flow, Ratio Analysis, Outstanding statements.

Tally shortcut yaad karne ki trick: F4 Contra (Cash-Bank), F5 Payment (Paid), F6 Receipt (Received), F7 Journal (Adjustment), F8 Sales (8 = "ate" = bech diya), F9 Purchase ("nine = kharida"), F10 Other/Memo. Yaad rakhne ka simple funda: 4-5-6 paisa aana-jaana, 7 adjustment, 8-9 bikri-kharid.

Basic steps — Tally me company banana

Step 1  Gateway of Tally > Company Info > Create Company
        -> Name, Financial year (1 Apr - 31 Mar), book beginning, currency
Step 2  Create Ledgers: Accounts Info > Ledgers > Create
        -> Capital A/c (Group: Capital Account)
        -> Cash A/c      (Group: Cash-in-hand)
        -> SBI A/c       (Group: Bank Accounts)
        -> Purchase A/c  (Group: Purchase Accounts)
        -> Sales A/c     (Group: Sales Accounts)
        -> Rent, Salary  (Group: Indirect Expenses)
        -> Ram           (Group: Sundry Debtors)
        -> Sohan         (Group: Sundry Creditors)
Step 3  Voucher entry karo (F4 - F9 ke through) as per transaction
Step 4  Reports dekho: Display More Reports > Trial Balance / P&L / Balance Sheet
Step 5  Backup: Company Info > Backup / restore data; period-end closing

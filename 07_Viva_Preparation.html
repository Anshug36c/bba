4. Management (CMM-102T) — 20 Ready Answers

QuestionAnswer (1-3 line)
Management kya hai?Lakshya achieve karne ke liye doosron ke through kaam karane ka process — planning, organising, staffing, directing, coordinating aur controlling ka combination.
Management ek science hai ya art?Dono. Science kyunki iske principles aur systematic knowledge hai; art kyunki application me skill, creativity aur personal judgement chahiye. Isliye "management = art of applying science".
Management ke functions (PoSTeDCoRB)?Planning, Organising, Staffing, Directing (Supervising + Motivating + Leading + Communicating), Coordinating, Controlling, aur Budgeting.
Management ke levels?Top (policy/strategy — CEO), Middle (department — branch manager), Supervisory/Lower (supervision — supervisor/foreman).
Taylor ka main contribution?Scientific Management — time study, motion study, standardisation, differential piece rate, "one best way"; also called Father of Scientific Management.
Fayol ka main contribution?14 Principles of Management (division of work, authority, discipline, unity of command/direction, equity, esprit de corps etc.) aur 5 functions; Father of Modern Management.
Unity of command kya hai?Ek employee ko ek hi boss se orders milne chahiye — do boss se confusion hoti hai.
Planning kya hai, steps?Future ke liye soch-samajh kar kaam decide karna. Steps: goal set → premises/prediction → options dhoondna → best chunna → plan banao → implement → follow-up.
MBO kya hai?Management By Objectives (Peter Drucker) — boss aur subordinate milkar goals decide karte hain aur performance usi goal se naapi jaati hai.
Decision making ke steps?Problem define → options → evaluate → decide/implement → review.
Organising aur organisation ka fark?Organising = process (kaam ko baantna, resources jodna); Organisation = structure/jad (kaun kahan baithta hai, reporting relationship).
Delegation of authority?Apna kaam/authority doosre ko sonpna — 3 elements: responsibility, authority, accountability (accountability delegat nahi hoti).
Span of control?Ek manager ke under kitne log directly kaam karte hain — narrow span = tall structure, wide span = flat structure.
Centralisation vs decentralisation?Centralisation = authority top level par; Decentralisation = decision power neeche tak. Decentralisation se speed + motivation + training hoti hai.
Coordination kya hai?Sabhi activities aur departments ko ek common goal ke liye jodna — isliye "coordination is the essence of management".
Control ka process?Standard set → actual performance measure → comparison → deviation analysis → corrective action.
Budget aur budgetary control?Budget = future ka financial plan (estimate); budgetary control = budget ke against actual performance compare karke control karna (variance analysis).
TQM kya hai?Total Quality Management — poori organisation ka continuous quality improvement approach; customer satisfaction + zero defect + employee involvement; jaise Toyota ka TQM aur "Kaizen".
CSR kya hai?Corporate Social Responsibility — Company ka samaj ke prati responsibility (Companies Act me 2% rule) — jaise Tata Trusts, plantation, education support.
Core competency kya hai?Wo khaas strength/skill jisme firm best hai aur competitor copy nahi kar sakta — jaise Sony ka miniaturisation, Amul ka cold chain network.

5. Business Economics (CMM-103T) — Ready Answers

QuestionAnswer
Business Economics kya hai?Economics ke principles + business decision-making ka milan — "economics in practice" (Applied microeconomics + some macro).
Micro vs MacroMicro = individual unit (ek consumer, ek firm, ek market). Macro = total (GDP, inflation, unemployment, national income).
Demand ka law?Other things same hote hue, price badhne par demand kam hoti hai — price ↓ demand ↑ (inverse relation), and vice versa.
Movement vs Shift in demand?Movement = same curve par price ke kaaran; Shift = poori curve left/right — income, taste, price of related goods, expectation, population, advertisement se.
Elasticity of demand?Price ke change par demand ka reaction: Ed = % change in quantity / % change in price. Ed >1 elastic, <1 inelastic, =1 unitary, =0 perfectly inelastic, ∞ perfectly elastic.
Kaunse factor elasticity ko affect karte hain?Substitute ki availability (zyada = elastic), income ka hissa, luxury vs necessity, habit, time period, multiple uses.
Demand forecasting ke methods?Survey (consumer/sales force), Expert opinion, Statistical (trend projection, moving average, regression), Test marketing, Leading indicator.
Production function?Inputs aur output ka relationship: Q = f(L, K, Ld, M...) — short run me kuch factors fixed hote hain.
Law of Variable Proportions?Short run: ek factor badhate jaao, baaki fixed → 3 stage: increasing returns → diminishing returns → negative returns (diminishing marginal product).
Returns to scale?Long run (sab factors badalte): increasing, constant, decreasing returns to scale (isoquant se samjhate hain).
Cost ke types?Fixed (rent), Variable (raw material), Total = FC+VC, Average = TC/Q, Marginal = ΔTC/ΔQ. Short run me U-shaped AC.
Explicit vs Implicit cost?Explicit = actual payment (salary, rent, bill); Implicit = opportunity cost (apna time/khud ki building ka rent aap nahi dete par maan lete hain).
Economies of scale?Bade production se per-unit cost kam — internal (production, marketing, finance) + external (industry ka growth, infra). Diseconomies = bahut bada hone par cost badhna.
Perfect competition ki visheshtaayein?Bahut buyers-sellers, homogeneous product, free entry-exit, perfect knowledge, price taker, firm ka demand horizontal; price = MR = AR.
Monopoly ki visheshtaayein?Ek seller, no close substitute, entry barrier, price maker; profit maximum par MR = MC; price discrimination possible (different price different customer).
Monopolistic competition?Bahut sellers par product differentiation (brand, quality, design) — jaise soap, shampoo, restaurant; short run profit, long run normal profit.
Oligopoly?Thoda sellers (2-10) ka market — interdependence, price rigidity (kinked demand curve), cartel ka risk; jaise telecom, airline, cement.
Price discrimination?Same product ka different price different customer/segment me — conditions: market segments alag, no resale, monopoly power (jaise student ticket discount).
Inflation kya hai, types?Price level ki lagatar badhotri. Speed se: creeping, walking, running, galloping, hyper. Kaaran se: demand-pull, cost-push.
Business cycle?Economy ka up-down cycle: expansion → peak → recession → depression → recovery.
BoP kya hai?Balance of Payments — ek desh ke saare foreign transactions ka statement: current account + capital account + errors/omissions; surplus/deficit.
Economic growth vs development?Growth = quantitative (GDP badhna); Development = qualitative (education, health, living standard + growth) — HDI se naapaa jaata hai.

6. Basics of Accounting (CMM-101T) — Ready Answers

QuestionAnswer
Accounting kya hai?Financial transactions ko record, classify, summarise karke interpret karna aur results (profit/loss + position) users ko batana (AICPA definition).
Golden rules of accounting?Personal: debit the receiver, credit the giver. Real: debit what comes in, credit what goes out. Nominal: debit expenses/losses, credit incomes/gains.
Double entry system?Har transaction ke do effect — ek debit, ek credit; dono hamesha barabar (Luca Pacioli).
Accounting equation?Assets = Liabilities + Capital.
Journal aur Ledger me antar?Journal = original entry, date-wise, narration ke saath. Ledger = final entry (principal book), account-wise, balance hota hai.
Trial Balance kya hai?Ek date par sab ledger balances ki list — debit total = credit total (arithmetic accuracy ka test).
Kaunse errors Trial Balance me nahi pakde jaate?Complete omission, error of principle, compensating errors, error of commission (dono taraf same galat).
Suspense account kya hai?Difference ko temporarily rakhne ka account; galti dhoondh kar rectification entries se band hota hai.
Trading A/c kya nikalta hai?Gross Profit/Loss = Net Sales − COGS (Opening stock + net purchase + direct expense − closing stock).
P&L A/c kya nikalta hai?Net Profit/Loss = Gross Profit + other income − indirect expenses.
Balance Sheet kya hai?Ek date par assets, liabilities, capital dikhane wala statement ("as on" date) — ye account nahi, statement hai.
Balance Sheet ke 2 format?Liquidity order aur permanence order (T-form); companies ke liye vertical/Schedule III format.
Adjustment kyun zaroori hai (outstanding, prepaid)?Accrual + matching concept ke liye — jo period me jiska faayda/nuksaan hua, usi me dikhana hai; isliye double effect (P&L + Balance Sheet) dikhate hain.
Depreciation kya hai? Kyun karte hain?Fixed asset ke cost ko uski useful life me systematic tarike se expense banana (wear-tear, obsolescence, time ke kaaran) — true profit + true value + replacement fund ke liye.
SLM aur WDV me antar?SLM = barabar har saal, original cost par (Cost − Scrap)/Life. WDV = book value ke % par, ghat-ti jaye; value zero nahi hoti.
Inventory valuation ka rule (AS-2)?Lower of cost and Net Realisable Value (NRV).
FIFO vs LIFO?FIFO = purana maal pehle (price badhne par closing stock + profit zyada); LIFO = naya pehle (India me financial statements me allowed nahi).
Receipts & Payments vs Income & Expenditure?R&P = cash basis, summary of cash/bank (capital items bhi aa jaate hain); I&E = accrual basis, surplus/deficit nikalta hai (NPO ke liye).
Tally me vouchers?F4 Contra, F5 Payment, F6 Receipt, F7 Journal, F8 Sales, F9 Purchase, Ctrl+F8/F9 credit/debit note, Ctrl+F10 memo.
Computerised accounting ke fayde?Speed, accuracy, automatic posting, instant reports (P&L, BS), GST compliance, storage, multi-branch, security/audit trail.

7. Business Environment (CMM-104T) — Ready Answers

QuestionAnswer
Business Environment kya hai?Wo sabhi internal + external factors ka sum total jo business ke decisions/performance par asar daalte hain aur jinke upar business ka control nahi hota.
Micro vs Macro environment?Micro = direct/close factors (customers, competitors, suppliers, intermediaries, financiers, public). Macro = broad factors (PESTLE + demographic + global).
Environmental scanning ke steps?Scanning (general info) → Monitoring (track) → Forecasting (predict) → Assessment (company par impact + strategy).
PESTLE ka matlab?Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental.
SWOT analysis?Internal Strengths & Weaknesses + External Opportunities & Threats — strategy banane ke liye.
ETOP/QUEST kya hai?ETOP = Environmental Threat & Opportunity Profile (factor-wise threat/opportunity + impact). QUEST = Quick Environmental Scanning Technique (managers ki brainstorming → scenarios → strategy).
Economic environment ke main factors?GDP growth, inflation, interest rate, money supply, fiscal policy, income level, exchange rate, taxation, economic system.
1991 ke reforms (LPG)?BoP crisis ke baad Liberalisation (licence raj khatm), Privatisation (disinvestment, de-reservation), Globalisation (FDI, trade opening, WTO) — New Economic Policy.
Liberalisation ka matlab?Government controls/compliance kam karna — licensing, price control, import restrictions hataana.
Privatisation ka matlab?Public sector ki role kam karke private sector ko dena — disinvestment, PSUs ka sale, private entry (Navratna/Maharatna concept).
Globalisation ka matlab?Economy ko world market se jodna — trade barriers kam, FDI, capital/tech/labour ka movement, MNCs ka entry.
FDI kya hai, route kaunse?Foreign Direct Investment = foreign company ka long-term investment with control; Automatic route (bina approval) aur Government approval route (sensitive sectors).
WTO kya hai? Benefits?World Trade Organization (1995, Geneva) — trade rules + dispute settlement; benefits: market access, MFN/national treatment, quota removal, dispute jeetne ka mauka.
Technological environment ka business par asar?Product design, production automation, e-commerce, cost reduction, new business models (Ola/Swiggy/Paytm), skill changes, global competition.
Technology transfer ke modes?Joint venture, licensing/franchise, turnkey, FDI, consultancy, reverse engineering, acquisition.
MRTP Act kya tha?Monopolies & Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1969 — monopoly concentration, RTP, UTP par control; 2002 me Competition Act ne replace kiya (CCI).
FERA vs FEMA?FERA 1973 = control (criminal offence); FEMA 1999 = management/facilitation (civil offence, compounding), forex transactions ke rules RBI ke saath.

8. Life Skills & Personality Development (CMM-105T) — Ready Answers

QuestionAnswer
Life skills kya hain?WHO ke anusaar wo abilities jo daily life ki demands/challenges ko positively handle karne me madad karti hain (adaptive + positive behaviour).
WHO ke 10 core life skills?Self-awareness, Empathy, Critical thinking, Creative thinking, Decision making, Problem solving, Effective communication, Interpersonal skills, Coping with emotions, Coping with stress.
Life skills aur other (technical) skills me antar?Life skills = behavioural/lifelong, har situation me kaam aate hain; technical skills = kaam-specific, training se seekhe jaate hain.
Hunter & Pigeons story se kya seekha?Unity is strength, leadership, quick decision, teamwork/coordination, dost (network) ka value, problem solving, positive attitude.
Personality ko shape karne wale factors?Physical (health, body), Social (family, school, culture), Psychological (motivation, emotions, intelligence), Philosophical (values, purpose).
Self-awareness kya hai?Apne strengths, weaknesses, feelings, values aur behaviour ki samajh — sabse pehla life skill; Johari Window (open, blind, hidden, unknown) se samjhaya jaata hai.
Empathy vs sympathy?Empathy = uski feeling ko uski jagah rakh kar samajhna; Sympathy = uske liye taras/afsos jatana; Altruism = uska bhala karne ke liye action.
Critical thinking kya hai?Evidence aur logic ke bina kuch na maanna — sawal karna, fact vs opinion check karna, phir conclude karna.
Creative person ki visheshtaayein?Curiosity, open-minded, imagination, original thinking, fluency, flexible, risk taking, persistence, observant, confident, divergent thinker.
Decision making ke steps?Problem → alternatives → evaluate → choose → implement → review.
Problem solving me root cause kaise dhoondein?"5 Why" technique — baar-baar kyun pooch kar asli karan tak pahunchna.
Stress kya hai, kaise manage karein?Demand vs capacity ka pressure. Manage: deep breathing/meditation, exercise, time management, 7-8 ghante neend, baat karna, hobby, healthy diet, positive self-talk, screens limit, professional help.
Mindset kya hai? Fixed vs Growth?Beliefs ka pattern. Fixed = talent janm se, failure = proof of weakness; Growth = effort se ability badhti hai, failure = feedback. Carol Dweck.
Time management ke essentials?SMART goals, prioritisation (Eisenhower matrix), to-do list, delegation, focus/no multitasking, "no" bolna, review.
Time wasters kaunse hain?Social media/reels, procrastination, perfectionism, unnecessary meetings, no-saying-no, poor sleep, multitasking.
Group aur Team me antar?Group = interaction + common goal, individual accountability. Team = complementary skills + shared purpose + mutual accountability + positive synergy.
Mature group ki visheshtaayein?Clear goal, clear roles, trust, open communication, shared leadership, issue-based conflict handling.
Tuckman's stages?Forming → Storming → Norming → Performing → Adjourning.

9. Computer (TCA-100T) — Ready Answers

QuestionAnswer
Computer kya hai? Kaunse 5 parts?Electronic machine jo input → process → output + storage karti hai: Input unit, CPU (ALU + CU + registers), Memory, Output unit, (System bus sabko jodta hai).
Data vs Information?Data = raw facts; Information = processed, meaningful data (DIKW: data → information → knowledge → wisdom).
Computer ki generations?Vacuum tube → Transistor → IC → Microprocessor → AI/ULSI. (ENIAC 1st, IBM 1401 2nd, IBM 360 3rd, IBM PC 4th, AI 5th.)
Charles Babbage kaun hain?Father of Computer — Analytical Engine aur Difference Engine design kiye.
RAM vs ROM?RAM = volatile, read/write, running programs (work desk). ROM = non-volatile, boot instructions/BIOS (permanent).
Primary vs Secondary memory?Primary = fast, costly, chhoti, RAM/ROM (volatile mostly). Secondary = slow(er), sasta, badi capacity, permanent (HDD/SSD/pen drive).
Cache kya hai?CPU ke paas ki super-fast memory jo frequently used data ki copy rakhti hai — speed badhati hai (L1/L2/L3).
Input/Output devices kaunse?Input: keyboard, mouse, scanner, mic, webcam, OMR/OCR/MICR, biometric. Output: monitor, printer, plotter, speaker, projector.
Software ke types?System software (OS, translators, drivers, utilities) aur application software (MS Word, Excel, Tally, customised software).
OS ke functions?Process, memory, file, device management, security, user interface, networking, error handling.
Compiler, Interpreter, Assembler?Compiler = poora program ek saath translate (fast run); Interpreter = line-by-line (easy debugging); Assembler = assembly → machine language.
Number system kaunse? (45)10 = ?Binary(2), Octal(8), Decimal(10), Hex(16). (45)10 = (101101)2.
Binary to decimal kaise?Har bit ko 2 ki power se multiply karke jod do — jaise (11001)2 = 16+8+1 = 25.
Logic gates?AND (dono 1 → 1), OR (koi bhi 1 → 1), NOT (ulta), NAND/NOR (universal), XOR (different par 1).
De Morgan's theorem?(A+B)' = A'·B' aur (A·B)' = A'+B'.
LAN/MAN/WAN?LAN = building/campus (fastest, private), MAN = city, WAN = country/world (internet).
Topologies?Bus, Star (aaj common), Ring, Mesh (most reliable), Tree/Hybrid.
IP aur MAC address?IP = logical address (badal sakta hai, routing ke liye); MAC = physical address (hardware me fixed, 6-byte).
DNS kya hai?Domain Name System — domain name ko IP address me convert karta hai (internet ki phone book).
ISP kya hai?Internet Service Provider — internet service dene wali company (Jio, Airtel, BSNL) jo IP + bandwidth deti hai.
HTTP aur HTTPS me antar?HTTP = plain (unsafe); HTTPS = encrypted (SSL/TLS) — login/banking ke liye zaroori.
Excel me cell, formula, VLOOKUP?Cell = row+column ka box (A1); formula "=" se shuru; VLOOKUP = table me value dhoondh kar required column se result laana.
Mail merge kya hai?Ek template + data list (Excel) se kai personalised letters/cards banana — Word ka feature.
PowerPoint me slideshow kaise start karte hain?F5 (beginning se) ya Shift+F5 (current slide se); Esc se exit; save .pptx/PDF.

10. Tricky & Bonus Questions — Smart Answers

Ajeeb/Tricky questionSmart jawab
"Accounting aur accountancy me fark kya hai?"Accountancy = poora subject/body of knowledge; Accounting = uske principles ka practical application (kisi firm me chal raha kaam).
"Profit hai to bhi cash khatam ho sakta hai?"Haan! Profit accrual par hota hai (udhaar sale bhi profit me hai), par cash actual me aane par milta hai. Isliye cash flow statement zaroori hai.
"Company profit kama rahi hai, phir share price kyun gir gaya?"Share price expectations, future outlook, market sentiment aur economy par depend karta hai — sirf past profit par nahi.
"Management art hai ya science?"Dono — principles science hain, unka use karna art hai (person-dependent).
"Ek dukaan me 2 boss (unity of command toot gaya) to?"Confusion, orders ki takraar, responsibility ka bhram — efficiency girti hai, conflict badhta hai.
"India me inflation badh rahi, business ko kya karna chahiye?"Cost control, price review (elasticity ke hisaab se), inventory planning, hedging, efficiency/automation, product value engineering.
"Aapko ₹5 lakh mil jaye, business me kaise lagayenge?"Answer me structure dikhao: market research (kitni demand), capital breakup (fixed + working capital), break-even, risk, ROI estimate — ye Business Environment + Accounting + Economics ka mix hai.
"Zindagi me sabse bada stress kab aaya, kaise handle kiya?"Ek honest example + kaunse technique use ki (time management/baat karna/exercise) + kya seekha — ye Life Skills ka best answer hai.
"Aapko decision kaise lena aata hai?""Main pehle problem samajhta hoon, options likhta hoon, pros-cons check karta hoon, phir decide karta hoon aur result review karta hoon — yehi decision making process humne padha hai." (Theory + personal touch)
"Naye paise (digital economy) ka business par kya asar?"Digital payments (UPI) se transaction speed badhi, cash leakage kam, formalisation badhi, online business (Amazon/OYO) easy hue, cyber fraud ka risk bhi aaya.
"Aapke college ko digital karne ka plan?"Wi-Fi + smart classes, ERP for attendance/admission/fee, digital library, cloud notes, biometric attendance, cyber-security awareness — plan do, technology ka bhi jawab.
"Business me ethical decision kaise?"Legal compliance, transparency (full disclosure), customer/employee fair treatment, sustainability, CSR — long-term me trust hi profit ka base hota hai.

11. Do's & Don'ts + Confidence Tricks

11.1 Viva se pehle (preparation)

  • Definition + 1 example har topic ka ready rakho — 15 second ka answer.
  • Apni self-introduction 10 baar loud bolo (aaine ke saamne ya phone recording).
  • Saare subjects ke basic formats ek page par likho: accounting equation, GP = Sales − COGS, Ed formula, PoSTeDCoRB, PESTLE, 10 life skills, gates truth table.
  • Ek "cheat sheet" banao (1-2 page) aur viva se pehle 10 minute me revise karo.

11.3 Confidence ke 5 practical tricks (nervousness ka ilaaj)

  1. 4-6 breathing: Viva se pehle 4 second andar, 6 second bahar — 5 baar. Body shant, awaaz steady.
  2. Power pose + water: Bahar 1 minute seedha khade raho, paani piyo (gala khulta hai, awaaz saaf).
  3. Pehla 30 second jeeto: Jaise hi andar jao — strong "Good morning Sir/Ma'am" aur safe question (self-intro) se momentum banao.
  4. Aankh contact ka 3-second rule: Bolte waqt examiner ki aankhon me 3 second dekho, phir nasheele/forehead par shift karo — stare nahi lagega.
  5. "Na jaanu" ko ticket samjho: Ek question ka nahi aata to next question pe full energy — examiner tumhare overall impression par marks deta hai, ek kadve sawaal par nahi.
Real life example: Ek student ko poora sem padha tha par viva me pehla sawal hi "define management" tha — usne ghabrahat me 30 second silence diya, phir examiner ne khud answer bola aur "next" kar diya. Confidence ke 30 marks chale gaye. Doosre din doosre student ko wahi sawal mila — usne kaha "Management ka matlab hai goals achieve karna doosron ke through — jaise ek captain 11 players ko lead karta hai. Isme 5 functions hote hain: planning, organising, staffing, directing, controlling." Same knowledge, alag presentation — 90+ marks. Viva me 70% marks tumhari delivery ke hote hain — isliye padhai ke saath bolna practice karo! 🎤
